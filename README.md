# PowerBI
Bajaj Two-Wheeler Sales Analysis 
This project presents a well-structured and comprehensive sales dataset covering Bajaj two-wheelers across multiple regions, states, cities, and time periods in 2025. It effectively captures key business metrics such as units sold, pricing, revenue, fuel type, engine capacity, and product launch year, enabling meaningful analysis of regional performance, model popularity, and revenue contribution.

The inclusion of diverse product categories—commuter bikes, premium bikes, and electric scooters—adds depth and allows comparison between traditional petrol models and EV adoption trends. Overall, the dataset is rich, realistic, and highly suitable for sales trend analysis, dashboard creation, and data analytics or business intelligence projects.
